The project.

LVXY Gaming and Entertainment is a personal project: a site for games, movies, and exclusive content, with a Beta Club that offers early access to unreleased games.

It's built on Wix and organized into a home page, a games section, a blog, an about page, a contact page, and the Beta Club.

What's on the site.

  • Home page

    A hero with a Beta Club announcement and clear buttons into the games and the club.

  • Games and blog

    Dedicated sections for the games and for blog posts.

  • Beta Club

    A page for members who want early access to unreleased games and exclusive content.

  • News

    A news area that points visitors to the LVXY YouTube channel.

  • Services

    Short sections explaining customer support, the games on offer, and fun challenges.

  • Contact and store details

    A contact page with FAQs, plus store hours and location in the footer.
